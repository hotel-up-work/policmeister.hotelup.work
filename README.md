## About
"Кава від поліцмейстера" is a cafe-museum in the Old Town of Kamianets-Podilskyi, located at vul. Starobulvarna, 8. The venue opened in 2006 in a historic Old Town building and combines a working cafe with a museum-like interior featuring old photographs and household items from different eras, including a restored coffee grinder. It is known for coffee brewed on sand, its own bakery, and author, home-style, and European cuisine, and it also has three hotel rooms on site for guests who want to stay the night.

## Amenities
- Coffee brewed on sand (natural coffee)
- Own bakery with daily fresh pastries
- Author, home-style, and European cuisine
- Kids menu available
- Museum area with historical photos, household objects, and an antique coffee grinder
- Space for private events (birthdays, anniversaries, family dinners, small corporate gatherings)
- 3 hotel rooms for overnight stays
